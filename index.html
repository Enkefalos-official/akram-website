RoboCAD
1st place · ENSA Kénitra · 10–11 December 2023

Winner as a 9th grader, on a university team.

RoboCAD is the robotics competition of the Club Mécatronique at the École Nationale des Sciences Appliquées de Kénitra. The theme was urban delivery, from design to build. I joined a university team in grade 9 and built the robot's electromagnetic lever.

MTYM
Regional finalist · Team SuperMaths · October 2026

Open research problems, written up and defended with my team.

For the 4th Moroccan Tournament of Young Mathematicians, four of us worked on Problem 1, "Grenouilles pêle-mêle": how frogs can sit on a ring of lily pads. We wrote the solution as a paper and presented it at UM6P.
